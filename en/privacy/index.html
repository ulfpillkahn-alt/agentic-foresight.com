2. Overview

This website does not use cookies or any analytics or advertising services that build user profiles. Personal data is processed only to the extent required to deliver the site, for the anonymous counting of visits and clicks described below, and to handle your enquiries.

3. Hosting via GitHub Pages

The website is provided via GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you access the site, GitHub processes technically necessary data in server log files, in particular your IP address, the date and time of access, the file requested, the previously visited page (referrer) and information about your browser and operating system.

The purpose is the secure and stable delivery of the website. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; the legitimate interest lies in a functioning and secure web presence. Data is transferred to the USA in the process. This transfer is based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, under which GitHub is certified, or on the standard contractual clauses of the European Commission used by GitHub. Further information: GitHub General Privacy Statement.

4. Contact by email

If you contact us by email, your email address, your name and the content of your message are processed in order to answer your enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your enquiry aims at a contract, and otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. The data is deleted as soon as it is no longer required for handling the enquiry and no statutory retention obligations apply.

5. Counting visits and clicks

To estimate the reach of the site, visits and clicks are counted with a counter of our own. It does not run with a third-party provider but on the web server of Prof. Dr. Ulf Pillkahn at pillkahn.de, which is hosted by ALL-INKL.COM – Neue Medien Münnich, owner René Münnich, Hauptstraße 68, 02742 Friedersdorf, Germany; a data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is in place. When a page is opened or a link is clicked, your browser sends a short message to this server. For technical reasons your IP address is transmitted; the counter does not store it, and the host logs the request in server log files that are deleted after seven days at the latest.

Only daily totals are stored: the number of visits; the host name of the page a visit came from (for example linkedin.com) or the note "direct" – not the full address of the previous page; for visits via a Google ad, the note "google-ads" instead (recognised by the click identifier Google appends to the target address; the identifier itself is neither read nor stored); the number of clicks on selected links and buttons and on external links (host name only). No cookies are set, no IP addresses are stored and no user profiles are created. Daily visit counts are deleted after 400 days, origins and clicks after 40 days.

So that a visit is counted only once, the page places a marker without any identifier in your browser's session storage (sessionStorage). It is deleted automatically when the tab is closed and is never sent to any server. The legal basis for counting is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; the legitimate interest lies in estimating the overall reach of the site.

6. Fonts and images

Fonts and images on this website are delivered from the same server as the site itself (GitHub Pages, see section 3). No third-party content such as Google Fonts, video platforms or map services is embedded.

7. External links

The website contains links to external sites, for example operation-zukunft.de, pillkahn.de, SSRN and others. Data is only transferred to the respective provider when you click such a link; that provider alone is responsible for any processing there.

8. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16 GDPR), erasure (Art. 17 GDPR), restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR) and data portability (Art. 20 GDPR). Where processing is based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, you may object to it on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR). An informal message to info@operation-zukunft.de is sufficient.

9. Right to lodge a complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The competent authority is the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (BayLDA), www.lda.bayern.de.
